Zero Tech · Company profile

Building the AI-native video studio.

Zero Tech is a Melbourne-based AI video company founded in 2024 by Hanna Zhu and Alex Liu. It builds accessible creator tools and cinematic production services for the next generation of video.

Founded 2024
Based Melbourne, Australia
Founders Hanna Zhu & Alex Liu

Company facts

What is Zero Tech?

Zero Tech develops products across the AI-video workflow: rapid motion-graphics creation, professional portfolio presentation for editors, and high-end AI brand-film production. The company is privately held and operates from Melbourne with a global creator community.
